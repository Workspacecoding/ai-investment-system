# Frontend Design

The frontend is a Vite React application focused on operational investment workflows.

Primary areas:

- Auth: sign in, session state, account security.
- Dashboard: portfolio summary, market state, watchlist, active plans.
- Market: universe search, ETF and stock detail, indicators, news.
- Strategy: scoring views, strategy candidates, scenario review.
- Portfolio: holdings, risk, goals, profit tracking.
- Reports: generated research and backtest outputs.
- Newsletter: editorial queue, generated issues, delivery status.
- Settings: data providers, broker settings, risk preferences.
